The system should achieve the following:
-Search for books using keywords, such as book title, author, or subject.
-Browse books according to subject categories.
-Display the book title, author(s), and other relevant bibliographic information.
-Indicate whether a book is available or unavailable.
-Display the exact shelf/location of an available book within the library.
-Allow authorized library staff to add new books to the database.
-Allow staff to edit, update, or remove book records.
Maintain an inventory of books in the library.
-Search the catalogue quickly without manually checking shelves.
